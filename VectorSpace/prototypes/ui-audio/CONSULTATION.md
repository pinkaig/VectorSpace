# Sound consultation brief: VectorSpace

Prepared 20 Oct 2026 for the audio consultation on 21 Oct. Everything here comes from the V3 mockup and the sound board built on it (see [README.md](README.md) to run it). The numbers are counted from `cue-map.json` and `bindings.json`, not estimated.

## 1. Who hears what

VectorSpace has three audiences, and they should hear three different amounts of sound.

| Audience | Where | What they should hear |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A visitor to a published museum | Play mode | Only the museum's own sound: room music, student reactions, footsteps, doors, card pops. No editor sounds. |
| A student building | The 4-step editor | All of the above while previewing, plus feedback for building: clicks, drops, step changes, rewards. |
| A teacher or VThere admin | Review, results, permissions | Close to silent. Confirmations and switches only. |

This is why the prototype puts editor sounds on their own `ui` channel, separate from the four channels the audio system already has (`bgm`, `sfx`, `narration`, `ambient`). A published museum can leave `ui` out entirely, and a teacher can mute it without touching the museum's music.

## 2. The sieve: 37 moments in 6 families

Every moment that can make a sound is a **cue**. 15 are P1 (the app feels broken without it), 16 are P2 (polish), 6 are P3 (nice to have).

| Family | Moments (examples) | Cues | P1 | Real audio today |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interface | any button, main-action button, commit button, close, switch on/off, pick an option, dialog opens, hover | 9 | 4 | 0 of 9 |
| Feedback | error popup, "Loading your room", status banner, a Mission-brief to-do ticks off | 4 | 3 | 0 of 4 |
| Editor | pick up a catalogue item, drop it, change step, switch view, zoom, rotate, undo, add a fact card, add a room | 9 | 2 | 0 of 9 |
| Publish and review | sent for approval, teacher approves, teacher sends back | 3 | 2 | 0 of 3 |
| Navigation | a whole screen changes, sign-in carousel | 2 | 0 | 0 of 2 |
| Play mode | Play and Stop, room music (two beds), fact card opens, next tour spot, tap to walk, footsteps, door, a student's own "play a sound" reaction | 10 | 4 | 1 of 10 |

"Real audio" means a cue plays a real recording from `public/audio`. Only room music "Museum hum" does. Every other sound (26 of the 27 in the map) is a synthesised tone, clearly marked `placeholder` on the board, so the app sounds complete before real sounds exist. No third-party sample pack is included. 14 of the 15 P1 cues are on placeholders.

### If we only get ten sounds

These cover every P1 cue:

1. Default button click (3 to 4 variants, so repeats do not sound mechanical)
2. Main action (purple buttons)
3. Commit (send, assign, save)
4. Close and dismiss (4 variants)
5. Error
6. Reward: a to-do ticking off, sent for approval, approved
7. Object dropped into the room
8. Step change (Place, Add info, Make it react, Play and share)
9. Play and Stop transitions
10. Fact card opening for a visitor

Plus two things that are not UI sounds at all: **room music** (the mockup offers "Museum hum" and "Soft piano") and the **student-facing sound library** that "then play a sound" reactions pick from.

## 3. What the placeholders are (and are not)

All 26 are plain tones generated by `tools/make-placeholders.mjs`, so the app sounds complete and every cue can be told apart. They were not designed and should not be judged as sound design. They are tuned only to be short, level-matched (peak about -13 dBFS) and distinguishable.

Things to ask the designer to deliver:

- **Short files with no silent tail.** Each play holds a voice open for the whole file, so trim tails.
- **One agreed level.** The engine can only turn sounds down (volume is capped at 1), so a quiet file cannot be boosted in code.
- **Variants for the sounds heard most.** The default click and the close sound each play several variants (two and four) so repeats do not sound mechanical.
- **Everything is open:** hover, clicks, close, error, toggles, drop, reward, transitions, footsteps, doors and music all need real audio.

Already in `public/audio` and usable now: a museum-foyer ambience (stands in for "Museum hum"), a comedic sad sting, a tip-toe sneak, a rock crash. The comedic sting is a real candidate for the error sound if the tone allows.

## 4. Proposed silence

Sound that is deliberately not there. Confirm or overrule:

- Typing in any text field, and scrolling.
- Dragging to look around or to move an object.
- Hover, if the answer to question 1 is no.
- Teacher and admin tables beyond switches, Save and Approve.
- Selecting an object (a soft tick is possible, but it would stack with the drop sound).

Every control that is not text entry already plays at least the default click, so there is no accidental silence. This was checked on 18 mockup screens.

## 5. Questions for the consultant

**Character**
1. Hover sounds: yes, no, or only on the main actions? Thirty students in one room make 30 hover ticks.
2. What is the tone for children aged 11 to 15 in a classroom: soft and wooden, bright and synthetic, playful? The error is the sharpest test of this: a cartoon fail, or a soft double bonk?
3. Should ticking off a Mission-brief to-do be the single "big" reward sound in the building loop?

**How the sounds should behave**
4. One gesture, one sound. Today an outcome replaces the click that caused it (the drop thud, not thud plus click). Is that the right rule, or should some moments layer?
5. Sound on press or on release? Press feels quicker; release matches when the action happens.
6. Room music: crossfade when a visitor walks through a door? Duck under a video pop-up or tour narration? **Neither is built in the audio system yet**, so the answer decides engineering work.
7. "Only nearby" (louder as a visitor walks closer) is in the mockup's reaction options. **Spatial audio is not built.** What falloff and what stereo behaviour?

**Content and licensing**
8. The student sound library: how many sounds, grouped how (by asset pack: Dinosaurs, Ancient Egypt, Outer space)? Who curates?
9. **Licence scope.** Published museums are shared by public link. Does the licence for the museum-foyer ambience already in `public/audio` (a Soundly clip) and any new sounds cover that redistribution?
10. Music: are "Museum hum" and "Soft piano" the right two beds, and what loop length is workable?

**Delivery**
11. One loudness target for all UI one-shots, and a separate one for beds. What do they recommend?
12. Format for production: WAV is fine for the prototype, but ten clips cost 3 MB, mostly silence.
13. Touch devices: no hover, and iPads in classrooms unlock audio differently. Anything to design for?

**Access**
14. Sound is never the only signal here (every cue has a visual twin). Is there anything else to build in: a visible mute in the product chrome, starting muted in a classroom?

## 6. Delivery spec to hand over (proposal, confirm with them)

- One file per sound, named after the cue it is for, with variants numbered: `ui.close_01.wav` to `ui.close_04.wav`.
- UI one-shots under 400 ms, hover under 30 ms, reward under 600 ms. Tails trimmed, no silence at the end.
- 3 to 4 variants for anything heard constantly: click, close, select, drop, footstep.
- Beds as seamless loops. Reactions as one-shots.
- 16-bit WAV at 44.1 or 48 kHz now; compressed formats later.
- One agreed peak level across the UI set.

To audition a delivery: drop each file on its row in the sound board. Nothing needs a rebuild.

## 7. A 5-minute walk-through that hits every P1 cue

1. Sign-in, then **Student** (screen whoosh, default click).
2. **Start building** (loading popup), skip, then **Next**, **Next**, **Let's build** (dialog pop, main action).
3. **Got it, let's build** closes the brief.
4. Pick **Dino skeleton** from the catalogue (pick up), then **Fits here, click to drop it** (drop).
5. **Add info** (step), **Fact card**, **Done** (info added).
6. **Make it react** (step, tip pops), **Make my first one**, pick **someone clicks it**, then **play a sound** (reward). Press **Try it** and flip Quiet, Normal, Loud.
7. Click the room name, then a **+** (door and reward), choose **Soft piano**, close.
8. **Play** (whoosh, room music), **Tour spots**, **Next spot** twice (card pop, ding), **Walk** then W A S D (footsteps), **Stop** (music fades).
9. **Publish**, **Send to Ms Tan** (sent).
10. **Sign out**, **Educator**, **Review 6 submissions**, **Approve and publish**.

Shortcuts: add `?screen=rule`, `?screen=playtour`, `?screen=publish`, `?screen=rooms` and others to the page address to start on that screen.

Every control on the page is outlined and numbered, coloured by the priority of the sound it plays (grey is the plain default click, red dashed is silent). The **On this screen** tab lists them by number; hover a row to find the control. Hover a row under **Sounds** to outline only the controls that play it. The kinds of sound and how they are managed are in [SOUND-CATEGORIES.md](SOUND-CATEGORIES.md).
