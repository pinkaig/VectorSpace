# Pilot and Conduct

**Status:** Requires DigiPen/VThere confirmation before external testing

## Usability pilot

The product is designed for a first-time teenage author with a fifty-minute lesson. Before Milestone 3, give the editor to someone outside the team without explanation. Observe the first two minutes, record where they hesitate, and convert findings into Jira defects or scope decisions.

## School-student testing

If the pilot involves school students, confirm consent, safeguarding, supervision, age-appropriate procedures, data minimization, and the institution's research/testing approval before collecting sessions or analytics.

## Confidentiality and assets

- Respect VThere commercial context and any shared partner material.
- Confirm whether an NDA is required before receiving real environments, assets, or partner data.
- Record each asset's license, attribution, permitted publication, and reuse terms.
- Do not publish partner data or student-identifying analytics without written approval.

## IP and handoff

Before final development, confirm in writing:

- ownership of student-built code, assets, designs, and outputs;
- permission to reuse or extend the application in a VThere product;
- publication rights for demo experiences;
- code and documentation handoff requirements;
- treatment of third-party and openly licensed assets.

## Analytics and privacy

Collect only the lightweight MVP events required by scope: visit, time spent, hotspot click, and scene reached. Define whether events are anonymous, how long they are retained, who can view them, and how an educator sees them. Do not add personally identifiable tracking without an approved requirement and privacy review.
