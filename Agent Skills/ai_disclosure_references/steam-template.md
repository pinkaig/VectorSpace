# Steam-Style AI Content Disclosure Template

Use only for game projects shipping (or practicing for) a Steam store page. Steam's policy disclosure requirements apply exclusively to AI-generated material consumed by players — artwork, audio, localisation, narrative elements, marketing assets, and content visible on the store page. Development tools used only for coding/efficiency (e.g. an AI coding assistant) are explicitly exempt and do not belong in this template — put those in the Singapore or EU template instead, or in the AI Declaration.

Fill every bracket. Delete subsections that don't apply.

### Live-generated AI content

Content generated by AI while the game is running (e.g. dynamic NPC dialogue, procedurally voiced responses to player input):

- **Feature:** [e.g. NPC conversation system]
  **What it generates:** [description]
  **Guardrails:** [what prevents the system from producing illegal or prohibited content — content filters, prompt constraints, moderation layer, etc. Steam requires this to be described, not just asserted.]

(If none: "This game does not use AI to generate content live during gameplay.")

### Explicitly out of scope for this disclosure

"AI tools used purely for development efficiency (e.g. code-completion or debugging assistants) are not disclosed here, consistent with Steam's exemption for tools that do not produce player-facing content. See the project's AI Declaration for a record of development-process AI use."

## Notes for the drafter (delete before finalizing)

- Don't let coding-assistant use bleed into this document — it doesn't belong here and including it just adds noise to a disclosure meant for players.
- The "guardrails" field for live-generated content is the one Steam actually scrutinizes closely — don't leave it vague ("we have safety measures") if the user can give specifics.
